A newspaper front page summing up Monday 23 November 2026's world news in a
single image — drawn with PIL in flat colours, no photographs.

## Stories covered

- **Lead — Climate talks collapse:** the UN climate talks (COP31) in Antalya,
  Turkey, collapsed on Sunday after the EU, the UK and other nations rejected
  a final draft that made no mention of fossil fuels — the longest COP on
  record and the first failure to agree a deal since Copenhagen in 2009.
- **The Gulf widens:** Yemen's Houthis say they hit Saudi Arabia's Rabigh oil
  refinery; the Saudi-led coalition says it intercepted a ballistic missile
  and drones; an Iranian strike on Bahrain wounded two people; two tankers
  caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz after hitting mines.
- **Libya:** suspected Ukrainian sea drones struck the Russian corvette
  Merkury at the Benghazi naval base; satellite images show it capsized and
  mostly submerged. Libya has asked the UN to investigate.
- **Ukraine:** a Russian drone hit a residential building in the Kyiv region;
  at least 14 people are missing under the rubble, President Zelenskiy says.
- **Indonesia:** a fire tore through a candy factory in Cikande, Banten;
  bodies were recovered and about 50 workers are missing, with the search
  resuming on Monday.
- **South Korea:** the education minister resigns over an AI-driven exam
  cheating scandal.
- **Nepal — India:** Prime Minister Balendra Shah holds bilateral talks with
  Prime Minister Modi in Delhi during his state visit.
- **Lisbon:** thousands march over the rising cost of living.
- **In brief:** UN talks on lethal autonomous weapons, North Korea, South
  Sudan, a concert shooting in California, cricket, chess, Israel, Brazil.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This copies the bundled `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
(default `/output`), verifies the copy has the same size, and prints the byte
count and SHA-256 hash. It needs no network access.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the front page (1024×1024 PNG)
- `main.py` — copies `entry.png` to the output directory
